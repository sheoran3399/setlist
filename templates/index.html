Setlist

SoundCloud sets, turned into Spotify tracks

Paste a SoundCloud link — a single track or a full DJ set — and this tool identifies every song in it using audio fingerprinting, then hands back the artist, title, and a direct Spotify link for each one. Long mixes (10+ minutes) are automatically sampled at multiple points across the recording, since a DJ set is dozens of songs blended together, not one.

Works with single tracks or long DJ sets/mixes.
